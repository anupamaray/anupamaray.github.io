About

I am a Senior Research Scientist at IBM Research, India. I work at the broader intersection of AI for Science and primarily focus on Quantum Algorithm Discovery.

Previously I have been working on deep learning algorithms for three major application paradigms: multimodal analysis, natural language generation and natural language understanding.

I completed my PhD from IIT Delhi, India. While in my PhD, I worked on developing deep learning algorithms and applying them for document analysis and computer vision applications. I am highly motivated and committed towards building AI solutions for social good applications.
